## Port Store 0.2.0

The first release of Port Store: install, update and remove ports right on a Miyoo Mini or
Mini Plus running OnionOS.

### Install

1. Download **PortStore-0.2.0.zip** below.
2. Unzip it to the **root of the SD card** (it contains `App/PortStore`; merge with the
   existing `App` folder).
3. On the Mini: **Apps → Port Store**.

Wi-Fi (Mini Plus) and the right date and time are needed for downloads. On a Mini
without Wi-Fi, put a port's release file in `App/PortStore/downloads` and install it from there.

### What's in it

- **60 ports and apps** from their authors' own releases, among them all 12 native N64 games
  of [Miyoo64](https://github.com/mr-finster/miyoo64) (bring your own ROM: Port Store finds it
  in `Roms/N64` and checks its version).
- **Games**, **Apps**, **Installed**, **Updates** and **More** tabs; genres on Select;
  pictures; groups like Miyoo64 under their own heading.
- Games appear in OnionOS's **Games → Ports**. Port Store offers to add Ports Collection when
  it is missing.
- Ports that need your files (Undertale, Fallout, Half-Life…) show what goes where and
  whether it is in place.
- Updates of ports, and of Port Store itself, under **Updates**. Removing a port keeps your
  saves and game files.
- Every download is checked against the catalog's SHA-256. The catalog is rebuilt every day.

### Controls

D-pad: move · L1/R1: tabs · Select: genre · A: open / install · X: remove · Y: refresh the
catalog / check files again · B: back, quit

### Known limits

- Only OnionOS, and only the 640×480 screen: the Mini v4 and Mini Flip are not supported yet.
- Ports packed as `.7z` or `.ipk` (OpenRCT2, Quake II) and releases laid out for
  other systems are listed under More, with a link, for installing by hand.
- After Port Store updates itself, start it again.

Catalog data from [Producdevity/MiyooMini-Ports](https://github.com/Producdevity/MiyooMini-Ports).
Thanks to every port's author.
