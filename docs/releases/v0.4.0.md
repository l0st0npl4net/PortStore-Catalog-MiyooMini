## Port Store 0.4.0

### MiyooPorts

Games from Port Store now have a system of their own in **Games → MiyooPorts**, with its own
icon, next to your consoles, whatever way their authors packed them:

- **Every game** shows there: also those released as apps (Minecraft, Petals), which used to
  stay in Apps. **Apps keeps only apps.**
- **No doubles:** the releases' own Ports shortcuts are kept out of sight, so a game shows
  once. OnionOS's own Ports entry keeps its games.
- **Games that need your files** show right away; started without them, they say what is
  missing and where it goes, instead of staying hidden.
- **Ports Collection** is added by itself when a game needs it.
- **Moving over is automatic:** the first start of 0.4.0 moves the games you installed before
  from Ports and Apps into MiyooPorts. Saves and game files stay where they are.

An icon pack can bring its own `miyooports.png`.
