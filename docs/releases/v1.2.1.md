## Port Store 1.2.1

A smoother screen: up to 60 frames a second instead of 30, and a port's page opening with the
same flicker as switching tabs.

### Install

1. Download **PortStore-1.2.1.zip** below.
2. Unzip it to the **root of the SD card** (it contains `App/PortStore`; merge with the
   existing `App` folder).
3. On the Mini: **Apps → Port Store**.

Coming from 0.4.1 or later: Port Store offers this update by itself.

### New

- **60 frames a second** while the screen moves: windows opening, text typed, the tube's
  moments, the phosphor's afterglow. Every effect keeps its own timing: a frame that comes
  late does not slow anything down.
- **A port's page** opens and closes like a new tab, with torn bands and flicker. The rolling
  picture is gone.
- **Both cores.** The tube's work on each frame is shared between the Mini's two cores. When
  it starts, Port Store measures whether that is faster and keeps the faster way.
- **Frame times in the log.** While something moves, `App/PortStore/cache/portstore.log` says
  every 10 seconds how long frames take (`portstore: frames: …`), and at start how many
  threads it uses (`portstore: tube: …`). Reports from a Mini are welcome: they show how close
  it comes to 60.

### Known limits

- **Not yet timed on a Mini.** 60 frames a second is the most Port Store draws. How many the
  Mini manages is not measured yet: the log will tell. If the screen stutters, set Effects
  to **calm** or **off** with L2.
- Everything else as in 1.2.0.
