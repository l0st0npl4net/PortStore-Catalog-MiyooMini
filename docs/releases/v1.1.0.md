## Port Store 1.1.0

Port Store gets a new face: a terminal on an old picture tube. Phosphor green, amber, ice,
white or red, scan lines and glow, the tube switching on and off, tearing and rolling as you
move between screens. It also gets a filter window for categories, and games from
[slop.kitchen](https://slop.kitchen), with their development builds when you ask for them.

![Games](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/l0st0npl4net/PortStore-Catalog-MiyooMini/main/docs/screenshots/games.png)
![A port's page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/l0st0npl4net/PortStore-Catalog-MiyooMini/main/docs/screenshots/port.png)

### Install

1. Download **PortStore-1.1.0.zip** below.
2. Unzip it to the **root of the SD card** (it contains `App/PortStore`; merge with the
   existing `App` folder).
3. On the Mini: **Apps → Port Store**.

Coming from 0.4.1 or later: Port Store offers this update by itself.

### New

- **The screen.** Everything in one phosphor's shades with the VT323 terminal font. You get:
  - windows with title bars, keys in brackets, buttons with the key in reverse;
  - pictures dithered in the screen's light;
  - a port's picture framed by rulers and cross hairs, its facts on a sheet with dotted leaders.
- **The tube.** Scan lines, glow and the phosphor's afterglow are always there. Moments are
  played as things happen:
  - the tube switching on at start and off on leaving;
  - bands tearing on a new tab, the picture rolling into place on a new page;
  - a degauss wobble on a new theme, a flicker when a window opens.

  While you use it, a slow band of light rolls down, the cursor blinks, and the picture
  glitches now and then.
- **System window (L2).** Theme (green, amber, ice, white, red, classic, or your own colours
  in `settings.json`), effects (full, calm, off), pictures (screen or colour), development
  builds. Kept at once.
- **Filter window (Select).** In Games and Apps, all categories with how many ports each has.
  Pick one, or all again.
- **Development builds.** Ticked in the filter (or the system window), the untested builds of
  catalogs that publish them show in the lists, marked `DEV`.
- **Porters' own catalogs.** Port Store now also takes games from catalogs in Mixtape's format,
  checked like everything else against their SHA-256. First in: **LAST LIGHT** by slop.kitchen
  (a survivors-like, in Games → MiyooPorts). Its development builds are LAST LIGHT DEV,
  UNDERLIGHT DEV and SOUND LAB DEV.
- **Boot screen.** Port Store types what it finds while the catalog downloads: card, catalog,
  installed ports, link. Any key skips it.
- **Event log.** A job shows each step with its time, a block progress bar and a spinner.
- **A port's page** lists what you bring first.

### Also

- Keep rules may be patterns (`settings.*`), as porters' catalogs write them.
- An app released as a shortcut for Ports now starts from Games → MiyooPorts instead of being
  hidden.
- Port Store's own entry no longer tells you to restart it by hand after an update.

### Known limits

- **Effects not yet timed on a Mini.** They are measured only on a computer and in an
  emulator of the Mini's processor. If they stutter on yours, set Effects to **calm** or
  **off** with L2.
- **OnionOS only,** on the Miyoo Mini family. The Mini v4 and Mini Flip (752×560) are laid out
  but not tried on the devices yet. Reports welcome.
- **Release files only:** a port's files must be a zip, tar, tar.gz or 7z on GitHub, or in a
  porter's own catalog. Others are listed under More, for installing by hand.
- **Your own game files** (for Undertale, Fallout, Half-Life, the Miyoo64 ROMs…) are yours to
  bring.
- **Downloads need Wi-Fi** (Mini Plus, Mini Flip) and the right date and time.
- **LAST LIGHT is a pre-release** (no sound yet). Development builds are untested by their own
  authors and may lose their progress.
